## 1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE
### 1.1 Purpose
This document defines the complete business model, revenue architecture, commercial relationships, pricing principles, commission structures, financial controls and monetization policies of PHERI NEPAL.
It establishes how PHERI NEPAL will generate sustainable revenue while providing genuine value to travelers, tourism businesses and other stakeholders.

### 1.2 Business Objectives
- Establish multiple sustainable revenue streams.
- Connect tourism businesses with potential customers.
- Generate revenue from successful commercial transactions.
- Provide paid digital services to tourism businesses.
- Develop affiliate partnerships.
- Offer optional premium digital services.
- Maintain transparent commercial practices.
- Support the long-term financial sustainability of the platform.
- Avoid unnecessary charges to travelers.
- Build a scalable tourism marketplace.

### 1.3 Relationship with Document 01
This document follows the project identity, scope, modules, revenue categories and operating principles established in Document 01.
It does not independently redefine platform features or technical architecture.

## 4. REVENUE STREAM SPECIFICATIONS
### 4.1 R01 — Accommodation Booking Commission
**Business Category:** Hotels, Resorts, Homestays and Guesthouses.
PHERI NEPAL may earn a commission from accommodation reservations generated through the platform.
**Commercial process:**
1. Traveler discovers accommodation.
2. Traveler reviews business information.
3. Traveler submits a booking inquiry or uses an approved booking integration.
4. Accommodation provider responds.
5. Availability and commercial terms are confirmed.
6. Booking is recorded.
7. Completed commercial transactions are reconciled.
8. Applicable commission is calculated according to the partner agreement.

**Revenue Recognition Rule:**
Commission must not be recorded as earned merely because a traveler submitted an inquiry.
The agreed commission conditions must be satisfied before revenue is recognized.

### 4.2 R02 — Tours and Packages Commission
**Business Category:** Travel Agencies and Tour Operators.
Revenue may be generated through:
- Domestic tour packages.
- International visitor packages.
- Cultural tours.
- City tours.
- Nature tours.
- Religious tours.
- Wildlife tours.
- Multi-destination packages.

Commission must be based on the agreed commercial terms and eligible completed transactions.

### 4.3 R03 — Transportation Referral or Booking Commission
**Business Category:**
- Private Vehicle Providers
- Taxi Services
- Tourist Bus Operators
- Jeep Services
- Domestic Transportation Providers
- Other Eligible Transport Services

Revenue may be earned through:
- Qualified referrals.
- Confirmed transportation bookings.
- Eligible completed transactions.

The payment basis must be defined in each commercial agreement.

### 4.4 R04 — Trekking and Activities Commission
**Business Category:**

- Rafting Operators

- Other Tourism Activity Providers

### 4.5 R05 — Travel Insurance Affiliate Commission

**Requirements:**

PHERI NEPAL must not present itself as an insurer unless legally authorized.

## 8. BOOKING AND REVENUE RECOGNITION
This section must comply with Document 01, Section 12.

### 8.1 Initial Booking Model
The initial operational model supports:
1. Traveler submits an inquiry.
2. Partner receives the inquiry.
3. Partner responds.
4. Availability is confirmed by the partner.
5. Commercial terms are communicated.
6. Traveler and partner proceed under the disclosed arrangement.
7. The booking outcome is recorded where evidence is available.

An inquiry alone does not represent a completed booking or earned commission.

### 8.2 Revenue Recognition Conditions
A booking commission may be recorded as earned only when:
- An eligible commercial transaction exists.
- The applicable partner agreement is valid.
- The commission calculation basis is known.
- The required transaction completion condition is satisfied.
- The amount can be reasonably determined.
- Refund and cancellation conditions have been considered.

The financial accounting treatment must be confirmed under applicable accounting standards.

### 8.3 Booking Status Separation
The system must distinguish:
- Inquiry Submitted
- Partner Responded
- Availability Confirmed
- Booking Confirmed
- Service Completed
- Cancelled
- Refunded
- Disputed

These statuses are not interchangeable.
A booking marked as confirmed must not automatically be treated as completed or commission-eligible.

## 26. VERSION 2.0 DETAILED COMMERCIAL SPECIFICATIONS & AUDIT GAP RESOLUTIONS

### 26.1 Anti-Leakage & Disintermediation Controls for Inquiry-First Bookings (`R01`–`R04`)
Because Phase 1 operates on an **Inquiry-First Booking Model** (`Traveler Inquiry -> Partner Response -> Partner Confirmation`) prior to automated online payment (`M15`), the following controls prevent off-platform commission leakage while delivering concrete incentives to both travelers and partners:
1. **PHERI Assurance & Verified Check-In Voucher (`VCV`):**
   - Travelers who complete confirmation through the platform receive an official **PHERI Verified Booking Voucher ID** with a 6-digit Check-In PIN / QR Code, emergency escalation support (`U06`), and Verified Review eligibility (`M13`).
   - Off-platform side deals forfeit PHERI dispute mediation, emergency support assistance, and verified review badges.
2. **In-Platform Relay & Masked Contact Progression:**
   - Initial inquiry threads (`M07`) take place inside the platform's tracked message ledger (`ENT-025`) with instant SMS/WhatsApp alerts to the partner.
   - Direct phone/WhatsApp handoff is unlocked only upon logged Partner Response or explicit booking arrangement tracking, ensuring the inquiry attribution timestamp is permanently recorded.
3. **Partner Conversion & Response Score Weighting:**
   - Partners who mark inquiries as "Rejected/Cancelled" while actually serving the guest off-platform are flagged by automated anomaly detection (comparing high inquiry volume against abnormally low confirmation rates and post-trip traveler feedback prompts).
   - High on-platform confirmation integrity directly boosts organic "Verified Reliability" trust badges (without violating the paid vs. organic separation rule).

### 26.3 Nepal Tax, VAT (13%), TDS, and Multi-Currency Accounting Rules
All financial calculations in `M15` and `ENT-027` to `ENT-033` must enforce Nepal statutory accounting rules:
1. **Tax Breakdown Separation:** Every invoice, commission calculation, and receipt must store separate columns for:
   - `gross_service_amount` (Base price before tax)
   - `service_charge_amount` (If applicable and explicitly disclosed)
   - `vat_amount` (**13% Value Added Tax** under Nepal Inland Revenue Department rules where VAT-registered)
   - `tds_withholding_amount` (**1.5% to 15% TDS** depending on service classification and PAN/VAT registration status)
   - `net_commission_amount` (Calculated on `gross_service_amount` excluding VAT, never double-taxing VAT)
   - `net_partner_payout_amount`
2. **Multi-Currency Snapshot Integrity:**
   - All accounting ledger entries must be anchored in **NPR (Nepalese Rupee)** as the statutory base currency.
   - When an international traveler views or transacts in `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `INR`, `CNY`, `JPY`, or `AUD`, the system must store `display_currency`, `display_amount`, `base_npr_amount`, `nrB_reference_exchange_rate`, and `rate_timestamp`.

### 26.4 Proof-of-Stay Verification & 72-Hour Dispute Resolution Protocol
To prevent false "No-Show" claims by partners attempting to evade commission:
1. **Dual-Confirmation Check-In:** Upon arrival, either the partner scans/enters the traveler's 6-digit Check-In PIN or the traveler taps **"I Have Checked In"** inside their Traveler Dashboard (`M11`).
2. **Automated Post-Check-In Survey Trigger:** 24 hours after scheduled check-in, if a partner marks a confirmed arrangement as `No-Show`, the traveler automatically receives an Email/In-App verification prompt (`"Did you stay at [Business Name]?"`).
3. **72-Hour Dispute Hold Window:** Commission eligibility (`ENT-029`) transitions from `Pending Stay` -> `Verification Hold (72 Hours)` -> `Earned & Payable` once no dispute is raised or once check-in evidence is confirmed.
