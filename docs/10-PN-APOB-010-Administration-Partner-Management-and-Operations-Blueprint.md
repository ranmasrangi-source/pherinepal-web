## SECTION 1 — DOCUMENT FOUNDATION

### 1.1 Purpose of This Document
This document defines the complete administration, partner management and operational framework of PHERI NEPAL.
It establishes how the platform will be controlled, managed, monitored and operated after implementation.
It specifies the operational responsibilities, administrative capabilities, partner workflows, approval processes, service management procedures and performance controls required to operate PHERI NEPAL as a national tourism ecosystem.

This document covers the operational side of the platform, including:
1. Central administration.
2. Administrative dashboard.
3. Administrative users and permissions.
4. Platform configuration management.
5. Traveler account operations.
6. Partner registration and onboarding.
7. Partner verification.
8. Partner account management.
9. Business listing management.
10. Partner dashboard.
11. Tourism service management.
12. Booking inquiry operations.
13. Booking arrangement oversight.
14. Customer support.
15. Complaint and dispute management.
16. Destination and content operations.
17. Multilingual content operations.
18. Review and reputation management.
19. Commercial operations.
20. Financial operations.
21. AI service oversight.
22. Notification management.
23. Security and privacy operations.
24. Operational team responsibilities.
25. Quality assurance.
26. Analytics and operational reporting.
27. Maintenance and service continuity.
28. Operational implementation and acceptance.

The purpose is to establish one consistent operational framework rather than separate, disconnected administrative processes.

### 1.2 Document Objectives
The objectives are to:
- Establish a centralized and controlled administration system.
- Define clear responsibilities for every operational function.
- Provide an organized partner onboarding and management process.
- Maintain reliable partner verification.
- Maintain accurate tourism information.
- Establish a consistent booking inquiry management process.
- Support travelers and tourism businesses.
- Protect user information and commercial records.
- Maintain transparency in subscriptions and promotional services.
- Establish operational oversight of AI services.
- Monitor system performance and service quality.
- Reduce fraud, misuse and administrative errors.
- Support national tourism coverage across Nepal.
- Prepare the operational structure for future growth.

### 1.3 Scope
This document defines what the administration and operations system must do and how authorized personnel must use it.
It does not replace:
- Document 01 for overall project scope and governance.
- Document 02 for the business model and revenue rules.
- Document 03 for user journeys and functional requirements.
- Document 04 for interface and design standards.
- Document 05 for technical architecture.
- Document 06 for database and data management.
- Document 07 for APIs and external integrations.
- Document 08 for AI services.
- Document 09 for security, privacy and legal compliance.

Operational requirements in this document must be implemented through the relevant modules, interfaces, APIs and data structures established in those documents.

### 1.4 Core Operational Principles
PHERI NEPAL operations must follow these principles:
- **Centralized Governance:** Platform-wide operational authority must be managed through controlled administration.
- **Defined Responsibility:** Every important operational process must have an assigned owner.
- **Least Privilege:** Personnel receive only the permissions necessary for their responsibilities.
- **Evidence-Based Decisions:** Verification, moderation and sensitive administrative decisions must be supported by relevant evidence.
- **Transparency:** Commercial relationships, verification, sponsored content and operational decisions must follow documented policies.
- **Data Accuracy:** Public information and operational records must be reliable and traceable.
- **User Protection:** Traveler safety, privacy and fair treatment remain priorities.
- **Human Accountability:** AI and automation must not replace human approval where human accountability is required.
- **Consistency:** Operational procedures must not contradict established project documents.
- **Scalability:** The operating model must support gradual expansion without requiring a complete redesign.

## SECTION 2 — OPERATIONAL STRUCTURE

### 2.1 PHERI NEPAL Operational Model
PHERI NEPAL will operate through a coordinated structure connecting:
1. Central Administration.
2. Platform Operations.
3. Partner Operations.
4. Verification Operations.
5. Tourism Content Operations.
6. Booking Operations.
7. Customer Support.
8. Commercial Operations.
9. Finance Operations.
10. AI Operations.
11. Technical Operations.
12. Security and Privacy Operations.
13. Marketing Operations.

These are functional responsibilities. They do not necessarily require separate departments or full-time employees during the initial operating stage.
One qualified person may manage multiple functions where appropriate, provided access permissions and approval controls remain separate for sensitive activities.

### 2.2 Central Administration
Central Administration is responsible for overall platform governance and operational coordination.
Its responsibilities include:
- Monitoring platform-wide activity.
- Managing authorized administrative accounts.
- Supervising operational teams.
- Reviewing critical reports.
- Approving designated operational decisions.
- Monitoring major partner issues.
- Coordinating security and compliance matters.
- Reviewing operational performance.
- Maintaining internal policies.
- Ensuring consistency across departments.

Central Administration must not bypass required approval procedures.

### 2.3 Operational Departments and Responsibilities
| Functional Area | Primary Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Platform Administration | Overall platform governance |
| User Operations | Traveler account support |
| Partner Operations | Partner onboarding and account management |
| Verification Operations | Evidence review and verification |
| Content Operations | Tourism information quality |
| Booking Operations | Inquiry and arrangement coordination |
| Customer Support | User assistance and complaints |
| Commercial Operations | Commercial agreements and subscriptions |
| Finance Operations | Financial records and reconciliation |
| AI Operations | AI quality and oversight |
| Technical Operations | Application and infrastructure support |
| Security and Privacy | Security and privacy coordination |
| Marketing Operations | Approved promotional activities |

### 2.4 Responsibility Assignment
Every operational process must have:
- A responsible function.
- An authorized decision-maker.
- Defined permissions.
- Required documentation.
- An escalation path.
- A review procedure.

Responsibilities must be documented before a process becomes operational.

### 2.5 Separation of Duties
Where practical, sensitive activities must be divided between different authorized personnel.
Examples:
- The person submitting a partner verification application should not be the sole approver of that application.
- A person requesting a sensitive financial adjustment should not be the only person approving it.
- A person changing privileged permissions should not independently approve their own privilege escalation.
- A content contributor should not automatically have unrestricted publication authority.
- A person handling a serious complaint should not silently modify the underlying evidence.

The exact approval hierarchy remains subject to formal operational approval.

- Business category.

- Operating location.
- Province.
- District.
- Contact details.

- **Step 5 — Initial Validation:** The system checks required fields and basic completeness.
- **Step 6 — Human Review:** An authorized reviewer evaluates the application.
- **Step 7 — Additional Information:** If information is missing, the partner receives a request for correction or additional evidence.
- **Step 8 — Decision:** The application is approved or rejected according to approved eligibility criteria.
- **Step 9 — Account Activation:** Approved partners receive access to permitted partner functions.
- **Step 10 — Listing Preparation:** The partner completes the public business profile and submits it for publication review.

## SECTION 7 — PARTNER VERIFICATION OPERATIONS

### 7.1 Verification Purpose
Partner verification is intended to establish confidence in the identity and legitimacy of eligible businesses and service providers.
Verification must be based on appropriate evidence and documented criteria.

### 7.2 Verification Requirements
Evidence requirements must depend on the business category and applicable legal obligations.
Potential evidence includes:
- Business registration.
- Applicable licenses.
- Permit information.
- Authorized representative details.
- Business address evidence.
- Contact verification.
- Relevant professional credentials.
- Other appropriate supporting documentation.

The platform must not assume that every partner category requires identical documents.

### 7.3 Verification Workflow
1. Partner submits verification information.
2. System records the submission.
3. Reviewer checks completeness.
4. Reviewer examines supporting evidence.
5. Additional evidence is requested where necessary.
6. Verification decision is made.
7. Decision and reasons are recorded.
8. Partner is notified.
9. Public verification information is updated where appropriate.

### 7.4 Verification Statuses
The system may use:
- Not Submitted.
- Pending.
- Under Review.
- Additional Information Required.
- Verified.
- Rejected.
- Expired.
- Suspended.
- Revoked.

The exact status transitions must be implemented consistently with Document 06.

### 7.5 Verification Independence
Verification must remain independent of commercial payments.
The following must not automatically grant verified status:
- Business subscription.
- Featured listing purchase.
- Sponsored content purchase.
- Advertising payment.
- Other commercial arrangements.

A verified business may also have no paid subscription.
A paying business may remain unverified until it satisfies verification requirements.

### 7.6 Verification Renewal
Where documents have expiry dates, the platform should:
- Record expiry information.
- Generate appropriate reminders.
- Request updated evidence.
- Route submissions for review.
- Update verification status when required.

### 7.7 Verification Suspension and Revocation
Verification may be suspended or revoked based on documented evidence involving:
- False documentation.
- Fraudulent identity.
- Expired or invalid authorization.
- Serious misrepresentation.
- Material policy violations.
- Legal or regulatory concerns.

The decision must be reviewed by an authorized person and recorded.

### 7.8 Verification Appeals
Where appropriate, partners should have an opportunity to request a review of a rejection, suspension or revocation.
An appeal must be assessed through an authorized review process.

## SECTION 10 — BOOKING INQUIRY OPERATIONS

### 10.1 Booking Operating Model
The initial PHERI NEPAL booking model is inquiry-based.
The platform connects travelers with relevant tourism partners.
The standard process is:
`Traveler submits inquiry -> Platform records inquiry -> Relevant partner receives inquiry -> Partner responds -> Traveler and partner coordinate -> Arrangement is confirmed or receives another outcome.`

An inquiry is not a confirmed booking.
A partner response is not automatically a confirmed arrangement.
A confirmed arrangement is not automatically a completed transaction.

### 10.2 Inquiry Management Responsibilities
Booking Operations must:
- Monitor inquiry flow.
- Identify routing failures.
- Monitor unanswered inquiries.
- Assist with communication issues.
- Record booking-related problems.
- Escalate disputes.
- Maintain operational visibility.
- Support accurate status reporting.

### 10.3 Inquiry Information
An inquiry may contain:
- Inquiry reference.
- Traveler reference.
- Partner reference.
- Service reference.
- Requested destination.
- Requested dates.
- Number of travelers.
- Service requirements.
- Traveler message.
- Submission timestamp.
- Current status.

Only information necessary for the inquiry must be shared with the partner.

### 10.4 Inquiry Workflow
- **Stage 1 — Submission:** The traveler submits an inquiry.
- **Stage 2 — Validation:** The system checks required information.
- **Stage 3 — Record Creation:** A unique inquiry record is created.
- **Stage 4 — Routing:** The inquiry is sent to the appropriate partner or approved workflow.
- **Stage 5 — Partner Notification:** The partner receives an appropriate notification.
- **Stage 6 — Partner Review:** The partner reviews the inquiry and available service information.
- **Stage 7 — Response:** The partner provides a response.
- **Stage 8 — Traveler Communication:** The traveler receives the response.
- **Stage 9 — Arrangement Coordination:** The traveler and partner coordinate the proposed arrangement.
- **Stage 10 — Outcome:** The inquiry is closed, withdrawn, declined, expired or linked to an appropriate arrangement according to approved rules.

### 10.5 Inquiry Statuses
Possible statuses include:
- Submitted.
- Received.
- Forwarded.
- Viewed.
- Responded.
- Awaiting Traveler.
- Closed.
- Withdrawn.
- Declined.
- Expired.

Final status definitions and transitions must follow Document 03.

### 10.6 Unanswered Inquiry Monitoring
The system should identify inquiries that remain unanswered beyond the applicable response target.
Operations personnel may:
- Send reminders.
- Contact the partner.
- Notify the traveler about delays.
- Record repeated non-response.
- Escalate persistent operational issues.

No fixed response-time guarantee is established by this document (see Section 35 Version 2.0 Operational SLA Matrix for approved target thresholds).

### 10.7 Arrangement Management
When the appropriate confirmation process is completed, an arrangement record may be created or updated.
Relevant information includes:
- Arrangement reference.
- Related inquiry.
- Traveler.
- Partner.
- Service.
- Agreed dates.
- Agreed conditions.
- Agreed commercial terms.
- Confirmation evidence.
- Arrangement status.

### 10.8 Arrangement Status and Payment Status
These must remain separate.
The system must distinguish:
- Inquiry status.
- Arrangement status.
- Payment status.
- Refund status.
- Commission eligibility.

An arrangement marked confirmed must not automatically be considered paid or completed.

### 10.9 Booking Cancellation and Modification
Cancellation and modification requests must follow approved partner conditions and applicable policies.
Relevant records must preserve:
- Requesting party.
- Request date.
- Reason where supplied.
- Previous arrangement details.
- Revised details.
- Outcome.
- Relevant financial consequences where applicable.

### 10.10 Booking Disputes
Booking Operations must coordinate disputes involving:
- Incorrect service information.
- Partner non-response.
- Service changes.
- Cancellation disagreements.
- Miscommunication.
- Suspected fraud.
- Other arrangement-related complaints.

Disputes must be escalated where necessary.

## SECTION 35 — VERSION 2.0 DETAILED OPERATIONAL SLAs, STALE INQUIRY ESCALATION & AUTOMATED RENEWAL WORKFLOWS (AUDIT GAP RESOLUTIONS)

### 35.1 Concrete Operational Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
To eliminate ambiguity in partner verification, inquiry handling, and customer support, all operational queues in `M16` enforce hard SLA timers:

| Operational Workflow | Responsible Role | Target SLA | Warning Threshold | Automated Escalation Action on Breach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Partner KYC Verification Review** | `U05` Verification Officer | **<= 48 Hours** (Business Days) | `36 Hours` (Amber Alert in `M16`) | Escalates to Senior Verification Queue + notifies `U08` System Admin. |
| **Partner Response to Traveler Inquiry** | `U03` Business Partner | **<= 12 Hours** (Ideal) / **<= 24 Hours** (Max) | `12 Hours` (Automated SMS/In-App Reminder to Partner) | At **24 Hours**: Second SMS alert. At **48 Hours**: Transitions inquiry to `Stale / Unanswered` and triggers **Traveler Alternative Partner Prompt**. |
| **Traveler Safety / During-Travel Support Ticket** | `U06` Support Agent | **<= 1 Hour** First Response | `30 Minutes` | High-priority badge in Support Console + instant alert to on-duty Support Lead. |
| **Standard Support / Account / Listing Ticket** | `U06` Support Agent | **<= 24 Hours** First Response | `18 Hours` | Escalates to Support Queue Supervisor. |
| **Flagged Review / Fraud Report Triage** | `U04` / `U06` / `U05` | **<= 24 Hours** Triage | `16 Hours` | Temporarily hides high-risk abusive content pending human decision. |
| **Financial Dispute / Commission Adjustment Review** | `U07` Finance Manager | **<= 72 Hours** Resolution | `48 Hours` | Locks payout item in `Dispute Hold` until documented resolution is logged. |

### 35.2 Automated Stale Inquiry Escalation & Traveler Protection Workflow (`M07` & `M14`)
To ensure travelers are never stranded when a local partner fails to reply:
1. **T+0 Hours (Inquiry Submitted):** Instant SMS (`INT-006`), Email (`INT-005`), and In-App notification sent to Partner (`U03`); Traveler (`U02`) receives Inquiry Tracking ID.
2. **T+12 Hours (No Partner Action):** Automated reminder SMS/Notification sent to Partner: *"Action Required: Traveler inquiry #[ID] for [Dates] is awaiting your response."*
3. **T+24 Hours (SLA Warning):** Inquiry flagged as `SLA_WARNING` in Partner Workspace and Admin Booking Monitor (`M16`); Support Agent (`U06`) can view and assist via phone follow-up.
4. **T+48 Hours (Auto-Escalation & Alternative Discovery):** If still unanswered, the system marks the inquiry `Unanswered / Expired`, logs a response-rate penalty against the partner's internal reliability score, and sends the traveler a curated list of **3 Verified Alternative Partners in the same district/category** with a one-click `"Forward My Inquiry to Verified Alternatives"` button.

### 35.3 Automated Partner Document Expiry & Annual Re-Verification Schedule (`ENT-017`)
To prevent verification bottlenecks as thousands of partners onboard:
- **T-30 Days Before License/Registration Expiry:** Automated email + dashboard banner prompting partner to upload renewed document.
- **T-15 Days Before Expiry:** SMS + Email warning; renewal submission enters priority `U05` fast-track queue.
- **T-7 Days Before Expiry:** Final urgent SMS notice.
- **T-0 (Expiry Date Reached Without Approved Renewal):** Automated cron job transitions partner status from `Verified` -> `Expired` (`ENT-015`), removes the `Verified Partner` badge from public cards (`DC-005`), purges the partner from AI recommendations (`AI-001`–`AI-016`), and logs an immutable audit entry (`ENT-040`).
