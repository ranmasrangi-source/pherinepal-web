## 1. PROJECT IDENTITY
### 1.1 Project Name
PHERI NEPAL

### 1.2 Project Positioning
Nepal's Trusted Tourism Ecosystem.

### 1.3 Brand Tagline
Discover Nepal. Travel with Confidence. Return Again.

### 1.4 Project Definition
PHERI NEPAL is a unified digital tourism ecosystem designed to connect Nepal's tourism destinations, travelers, tourism businesses, local communities, service providers and relevant institutions through one integrated platform.
The platform will provide reliable tourism information, discovery tools, travel assistance, business services and digital tourism solutions.
It will support travelers throughout three major stages:
- Before Travel
- During Travel
- After Travel

Its long-term objective is to develop into a comprehensive national tourism platform connecting Nepal's tourism industry with international markets.

## 5. TWELVE-LANGUAGE SYSTEM

PHERI NEPAL must support 12 languages.

## 29. VERSION 2.0 DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS & AUDIT GAP RESOLUTIONS

### 29.1 7-Province and 77-District Phased Content Readiness & Anti-Empty-Page Governance
To resolve the "77-District Cold-Start Problem" while fulfilling the national mandate across all 7 provinces and 77 districts of Nepal, every district record must operate under a 3-Tier Content Readiness Matrix:
