## 1. DOCUMENT FOUNDATION

### 1.1 Purpose
This document defines the marketing, search engine optimization (SEO), content marketing, audience acquisition, brand communication, partnership marketing, user engagement, and sustainable growth strategy for PHERI NEPAL.
It establishes a structured approach to introducing PHERI NEPAL to domestic and international travelers, building public trust, attracting tourism businesses, increasing platform visibility, and developing long-term relationships with travelers and tourism stakeholders.

### 1.2 Strategic Alignment
This document must remain consistent with:
- Document 01 — Master Project Blueprint
- Document 02 — Business Model and Revenue Blueprint
- Document 03 — User Journey and Functional Requirements Blueprint
- Document 04 — UI/UX and Design System Blueprint
- Document 05 — Technical Architecture Blueprint
- Document 06 — Database and Data Management Blueprint
- Document 07 — API and Third-Party Integration Blueprint
- Document 08 — AI and Intelligent Services Blueprint
- Document 09 — Security, Privacy and Legal Compliance Blueprint
- Document 10 — Administration, Partner Management and Operations Blueprint

Marketing activities must reflect the actual operational capabilities of the platform. Planned features must not be advertised as fully operational services.

### 1.3 Core Marketing Objective
Establish PHERI NEPAL as a trusted digital platform through which travelers can discover Nepal, explore destinations, find tourism services, communicate with verified partners, prepare their journeys, and remain connected after travel.

## 4. TARGET AUDIENCE AND MARKET SEGMENTATION

## 6. MARKETING FUNNEL AND TRAVELER LIFECYCLE

Marketing must support the three travel stages established in Document 01.

### 6.1 Before Travel

- Help travelers discover destinations.

- Support travel preparation.
- Encourage exploration of tourism services.
- Generate qualified inquiries.
- Build confidence before travel.

### 6.2 During Travel
Primary marketing and engagement objectives:
- Help travelers discover nearby destinations and services.
- Provide access to relevant tourism information.
- Encourage appropriate use of AI travel assistance.
- Support communication with tourism partners.
- Promote responsible local experiences.
- Maintain useful platform engagement.

### 6.3 After Travel
Primary marketing and engagement objectives:
- Encourage travelers to share genuine experiences.
- Support review submission.
- Maintain relationships with previous travelers.
- Promote relevant future travel information.
- Encourage referrals where appropriate.
- Support repeat visits and return travel.

### 6.4 Marketing Funnel
The marketing funnel consists of:
1. **Awareness** — Travelers discover PHERI NEPAL.
2. **Interest** — Travelers explore tourism information.
3. **Consideration** — Travelers compare destinations and services.
4. **Engagement** — Travelers interact with relevant platform features.
5. **Inquiry** — Travelers contact tourism partners through available inquiry processes.
6. **Experience** — Travelers use the platform during their journey.
7. **Retention** — Travelers return to use relevant services.
8. **Advocacy** — Travelers share genuine experiences and recommend the platform.

A website visit, registration, inquiry, confirmed arrangement, completed travel service, and commercial transaction must be measured as separate events.

## 8. SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMIZATION (SEO)

### 8.1 SEO Objective
Make accurate and useful PHERI NEPAL tourism pages discoverable through major search engines.
SEO must prioritize genuine user value, technical quality, content accuracy, and long-term visibility.

### 8.2 SEO Governance
SEO activities must follow the technical architecture in Document 05 and the API and integration governance in Document 07.
SEO implementation must not introduce unauthorized changes to platform architecture or content governance.

### 8.3 Technical SEO
The website should support:
- Search-engine crawlability.
- Appropriate indexability controls.
- XML sitemaps.
- Robots.txt configuration.
- Canonical URLs.
- Logical URL structures.
- Mobile-friendly rendering.
- Responsive page layouts.
- HTTPS.
- Appropriate redirect handling.
- Correct HTTP status codes.
- Optimized page performance.
- Structured metadata.
- Image optimization.
- Internal linking.
- Duplicate-content prevention.
- Search-engine-friendly rendering.

Private user accounts, partner dashboards, administrative interfaces, private inquiries, and confidential records must not be exposed through public indexing.

### 8.4 URL Structure
Public URLs should be:
- Readable.
- Consistent.
- Descriptive.
- Stable where possible.
- Language-aware where required.
- Free from unnecessary parameters.

Illustrative URL patterns:
- `/destinations/`
- `/destinations/pokhara/`
- `/destinations/annapurna-region/`
- `/accommodation/`
- `/activities/`
- `/travel-guides/`

These are proposed patterns, not finalized production routes.
Final routing must be approved through the technical implementation process.

### 8.5 On-Page SEO
Each public content page should have:
- A unique page title.
- A useful meta description.
- One clear primary heading.
- Logical heading hierarchy.
- Relevant page content.
- Descriptive image alternative text.
- Relevant internal links.
- Appropriate canonical configuration.
- Accurate location and service information.

Keyword usage must remain natural and must not compromise readability.

### 8.6 Local and Destination SEO
Destination pages should include relevant information such as:
- Destination name.
- Province and district.
- Geographic context.
- Main attractions.
- Activities.
- Travel considerations.
- Relevant tourism services.
- Verified contact or service information where appropriate.
- Relevant images.
- Useful related destinations.

Location information must be checked before publication.

### 8.7 Structured Data
Where appropriate, the platform may implement supported structured data for:
- Organization.
- Website.
- Breadcrumbs.
- Articles.
- Tourist attractions.
- Local businesses.
- Relevant events.
- Other eligible public content types.

Structured data must accurately represent visible page content.
It must not be used to create false ratings, reviews, prices, availability, or business details.

### 8.8 Multilingual SEO
The platform's multilingual architecture must support language-specific SEO.
Requirements include:
- Language-specific page content.
- Appropriate language metadata.
- Correct canonical configuration.
- Appropriate hreflang implementation where applicable.
- Consistent translated page relationships.
- Language-specific metadata.
- Correct language switching.
- Prevention of misleading machine-translated content.

Only Nepali and English are foundational languages at the initial planning level.
The remaining 10 languages must be selected through verified international source-market and language-demand research.

### 8.9 SEO Content Quality
SEO content must:
- Be useful and original.
- Provide accurate tourism information.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Avoid misleading claims.
- Avoid mass-produced low-quality pages.
- Identify uncertainty where relevant.
- Be reviewed before publication.
- Be updated when information changes.

### 8.10 SEO Monitoring
SEO performance should be monitored through approved analytics and search tools.
Monitoring areas include:
- Indexed pages.
- Search impressions.
- Search clicks.
- Search click-through rate.
- Search ranking trends.
- Organic landing-page traffic.
- Technical indexing errors.
- Broken links.
- Core Web Vitals.
- Organic inquiry conversions.

Search ranking positions must not be guaranteed.

## 34. VERSION 2.0 DETAILED SEO, SCHEMA.ORG & NEPAL SEASONAL GROWTH SPECIFICATIONS (AUDIT GAP RESOLUTIONS)

### 34.1 Anti-Thin-Content & Programmatic SEO Indexing Thresholds (77 Districts x 12 Languages)
To prevent search engine penalties ("Thin Content" or "Doorway Page" demotions) across `77 districts x 12 languages = 924+` potential URL combinations:
1. **Strict Indexation Gate (`robots` Meta & Sitemap Rule):**
   - A localized destination or district URL (`/:lang/districts/:slug` or `/:lang/destinations/:slug`) is granted `<meta name="robots" content="index, follow">` and included in `/sitemap.xml` **ONLY IF**:
     1. It belongs to **Readiness Tier A or Tier B** (`>= 400 words` of verified, structured content + elevation/season/access metadata), **AND**
     2. Its translation status in that language (`ENT-011`) is **`Approved`** or **`Published`** (human-reviewed or quality-verified).
   - Pages in **Readiness Tier C** or with **`Not Started` / unreviewed `AI Draft`** translation status remain accessible to human users inside the UI with a clear `"Translation in Progress / Foundation Profile"` badge, but automatically emit `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` so search engine crawl budget is concentrated 100% on high-quality verified pages.
2. **Canonical & `hreflang` Cluster Integrity:**
   - Every indexed page emits bidirectional `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="ne-NP">`, `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-US">`, and `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default">` tags pointing exclusively to active, index-eligible language versions.

### 34.2 Complete Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data Architecture
Every public template must inject validated JSON-LD structured data matching its exact tourism domain entity:

| Page / Entity Type | Primary Schema.org `@type` | Required Properties & Trust Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Platform Home & Brand** | `Organization` + `WebSite` (`SearchAction`) | Official name `"PHERI NEPAL"`, logo, tagline, and site search URL template. |
| **Province & District Hubs (`M02`)** | `AdministrativeArea` + `TouristDestination` + `BreadcrumbList` | GeoCoordinates, elevation, contained attractions, `touristType`, and province hierarchy. |
| **Destination Detail Page (`M02`)** | `TouristDestination` + `Place` + `FAQPage` | Latitude/longitude, best time to visit, permit requirements, and verified safety FAQs. |
